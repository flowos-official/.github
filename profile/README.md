## English

FlowOS specializes in AI transformation (AX). We connect business data and build custom AI systems and workflow automation, starting with the ERP, MES, spreadsheets and documents a company already uses.

| Solution | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [Context Hub](https://flowos.work/en/context-hub) | Connected company information |
| [AI Layer](https://flowos.work/en/ai-layer) | Company-specific AI tools |
| [OS-Pack](https://flowos.work/en/os-pack) | Business operations system |
